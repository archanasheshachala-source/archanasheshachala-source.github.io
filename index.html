From molecule to device

I study how organic materials extract positive charge (holes) from perovskite solar cells.

19.24% efficiency with dopant-free hole transport

Carbazole–triphenylamine hybrids · First author · Carbon Energy, accepted

Collaborative work with Penn State, USA.

I designed star-shaped carbazole–triphenylamine materials for hole extraction without added dopants.

Unencapsulated SS1 cells retained >91% of their initial efficiency after 1,500 h of storage (~28 °C, ~65% relative humidity).

Contribution & methods

My contribution: Molecular design and synthesis, with experimental and theoretical analysis connecting structure, charge transport, and device performance.

SS1 and SS2 share a carbazole core with two thiophene-linked triphenylamine arms and an N-linked methoxy-substituted triphenylamine arm. Methoxy substitution on the two terminal arms distinguishes SS1 from SS2; the N-linked arm is not a fully conjugated extension of the backbone. In small-area MAPbI₃ cells (~0.096 cm²), SS1 reached 17.73% PCE. The separate mixed-cation/mixed-halide cell reached 19.24%, and a 1 cm² MAPbI₃ cell reached 17.25%. Unencapsulated SS1 cells retained >91% of initial PCE after 1,500 h of storage (~28 °C, ~65% RH).
